Why Railflow

Built for transparent testnet trading workflows

LIVE

Reference data first

Market prices, funding, open interest, and volume are sourced from Binance Futures. Values are shown as reference data until Railflow execution is connected.

USDC

Clear margin workflow

Deposit native USDC into the Arc Testnet vault, then review available collateral before using the trading workspace.

No KYC

Wallet-native access

No email account is required. Wallet balances, vault collateral, and indexed transactions are read from your connected Arc Testnet address.

Live market data

Binance Futures WebSocket and REST data power live prices, funding, depth, and reference metrics.

On-chain collateral

Vault deposits and withdrawals are signed by your wallet on Arc Testnet.

USDC settlement

Arc native USDC is the collateral and gas asset used by this testnet environment.

Transparent status

Unavailable protocol data is shown as pending or unavailable instead of being estimated.
