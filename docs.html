What is Railflow?

Railflow is an Arc Testnet trading workspace with on-chain USDC collateral, live market references, lending actions, vault accounting, and wallet-based portfolio history.

What can you do on Railflow?

TRADE

Review live Binance Futures reference prices, funding, market depth, and candlestick data in the trading workspace.

COLLATERAL

Deposit and withdraw USDC through the Collateral Vault using your connected Arc Testnet wallet.

LEND

Supply or borrow native USDC through the Arc Testnet money-market contract when the pool data is available.

PORTFOLIO

Review wallet balances and indexed on-chain transaction history for the connected address.

NetworkArc Testnet · Chain ID 5042002 · native USDC gas environment
02

Trading workspace

Trade is a wallet-connected interface for reviewing perpetual market data and managing a local trading workspace. Market references come from Binance Futures; they are not an Arc execution oracle.

Market data

The workspace can display mark price, 24-hour change, funding, open interest, volume, order-book depth, and live candlesticks. WebSocket streams are preferred, with REST polling used when a stream reconnects.

Orders and position tools

01Choose a market, side, order type, size, margin mode, and leverage.
02Set optional take-profit and stop-loss trigger prices.
03Review estimated margin, liquidation price, and fees before submitting.
Execution statusFutures matching, settlement, and liquidation are not connected to a live Railflow protocol engine yet. The current order ledger is explicitly marked simulated.
03

Collateral Vault

The Collateral Vault is the funding layer for the trading workspace. It moves native USDC between your connected wallet and the Railflow vault contract on Arc Testnet.

Deposit flow

01Connect an EVM wallet and switch to Arc Testnet.
02Open Deposit from the trading header and enter a USDC amount.
03Approve USDC, then confirm the vault deposit transaction.
04Return to Trade and review the available collateral balance.

Vault statistics

The Vault chart is reserved for on-chain vault statistics: Cumulative PnL, Daily PnL, Daily APR, and Vault Equity. Values stay unavailable until the corresponding strategy data source is live; no return estimate is fabricated.

04

Lending

Railflow Lending connects Supply and Borrow actions to the Arc Testnet money-market contract. The current interface focuses on native USDC.

Supply

Supplying USDC adds liquidity to the money market and may earn variable interest when borrowers use the pool. The wallet signs the approval and supply transactions.

Borrow

Borrowing requires available collateral and pool liquidity. The interface validates the entered amount and displays capacity only when the contract returns the required data.

Rate dataPool balances and utilization are read on-chain where available. Historical lending candles and APY history remain pending until a rate-history source is connected.
05

Portfolio

Portfolio follows the connected wallet instead of inventing account values. It combines wallet balances, Arc Testnet contract reads, and indexed transaction history.

What is shown?

LIVE BALANCES

Native USDC and supported wallet balances are read from the connected address.

HISTORY

Deposits, withdrawals, supplies, borrows, and other indexed transactions appear when the explorer indexer provides them.

Indexer dependencyIf the ArcScan indexer is delayed or unavailable, transaction history is shown as unavailable rather than reconstructed from guesses.

Risks & FAQ

Is Railflow production-ready?

No. Railflow is an Arc Testnet dApp. It is for testing and interface development, not for real funds.

Are returns guaranteed?

No. Vault returns, lending rates, and trading outcomes are variable and may be unavailable. Never treat a displayed reference price as a liquidation oracle.

What happens if a data source stops?

Live market panels retry through a fallback source and show an unavailable state if no current data can be verified. Protocol metrics remain pending until their source is connected.

Risk reminderTestnet contracts may be upgraded, paused, or reset. Use only assets you are comfortable using for development and testing.
